The Hall of Faces is my own creation (MOC) life-sized LEGO rendition of the wall of cubbies keeping faces for impersonation for the assassin's guild of HBO's [Game of Thrones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_Thrones) television series. It is a companion piece to my [Lego Throne](https://thronebuilder.com/blog/lego-throne/).

The show's [Hall of Faces](https://gameofthrones.fandom.com/wiki/Hall_of_Faces) is referenced in show posters featuring cast members and was parodied with those actors on [The Late Late Show with James Corden](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pk7E5HNqgxY).

The original set of face mosaics shown in this image are of members of my household, generated with https://lego-art-remix.com. I am building [Faceplate](https://thronebuilder.com/blog/faceplate), a customized generator at https://faceplate.me, to crowdsource future mosaics but am still tuning the algorithm and the uncanny vally of the virtual [Hall of Faces](https://faceplate.me/hall-of-faces).


# History

## Version 1

Immediately after exhibiting at BrickCon 2025 in September 2025, I was considering my next project when my friends Stephan and Scott hit upon the show's Hall of Faces as an ideal successor to the throne and window.

I figured the face mosaics would be the riskiest part of the build, and so immediately started taking pictures of faces to cast and putting them into https://lego-art-remix.com to generate mosaics.
